Ash Valley

The volcano is waking up, and its ash is dangerous to breathe. Keep the villagers alive!

How to play
  • Drag anywhere on the map to move. A joystick pops up under your finger. Drag a little to walk, or farther to run.
  • Use ⟲ ⟳ in the corner to spin the camera around.
  • Tap right next to you (or press Use) to chop 🌲, mine 🪨, and sweep ash.
  • If a villager faints, stand next to their house and press Use 5 times to wake them up. Full-heal magic words wake everyone up too.
  • Build 🌀 air filters to clean ash, and 🛡️ shelters to protect houses.
  • Grab 📜 scrolls to learn magic words. Longer words have bigger powers. Once you learn a word it’s yours forever, it just needs to recharge.
  • The valley is big! Use the mini map in the corner to see which houses are in trouble.
  • There’s a legendary word out there. It’s 189,819 letters long…
