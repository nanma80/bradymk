FlatCraft

A 2D Minecraft in your browser.

Mine blocks. Craft tools. Build anything.

Press Space or click to continue

Movement

WASD or Arrow Keys to move

Space or Up Arrow to jump

Mining & Building

Left Click hold to mine blocks

Right Click to place blocks

1-9, Scroll, or Shift+, / Shift+. to select hotbar slot

Crafting

Press E or Tab for hand crafting (planks, sticks, crafting table)

Right Click a Crafting Table for tools, weapons, and more

Punch trees for wood. Wood makes planks. Planks make sticks and a table.

Modes

C toggles Creative / Survival

Creative: F to fly, infinite blocks, press E to browse pages

Survival: mine, craft, and build from scratch

Good to know

Right Click TNT with a torch to light it

Ladders let you climb. Better pickaxes mine better ores.

Shift+, / Shift+. to scroll hotbar slots

Redstone

Craft Redstone Wire, Levers, Buttons, and Redstone Torches at a Crafting Table.

Place Levers and Buttons on solid blocks or in air. Right Click to toggle/press.

Wire carries signal up to 15 blocks. Repeaters boost signal back to 15.

Doors open with Redstone power or Right Click. Pistons push blocks when powered.

Sticky Pistons pull blocks back when retracting. Observers detect block changes and emit a redstone pulse.

Press Space to play!
